Frequently Asked Questions

What are the eligibility requirements for a loan?expand_more

Eligibility: Salaried individuals, Sole Proprietors, Farming Co-operatives, MSMEs, Women Groups, and enterprising Youth.

  • Valid ID (National ID, Passport, or Driver's License)
  • Proof of Residence (utility bill) and proof of business existence (e.g., registration certificate)
  • Proof of Income (payslips for salaried individuals; bank or financial statements for businesses)
How long does the approval process take?expand_more

Where all valid and relevant documentation is submitted, approval is completed within 72 hours (3 working days) for branch/OTC applications and within 5 working days for field-agent applications

Are there any hidden fees?expand_more

Transparent fees include standard processing fees, an administration fee (covering Credit Reference Bureau checks, distribution, and communications), an insurance premium (~1% of loan amount), and legal fees where pledged asset registration or title changes apply.

Can I settle my loan early?expand_more

Yes, borrowers can settle their loans early at any time without incurring any penalty fees.
