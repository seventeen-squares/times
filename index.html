Frequently Asked Questions

What are the eligibility requirements for a loan? expand_more
Eligibility depends on the loan type. Generally, you need to be a Malawian resident, 18+ years old, and have a steady source of income (salary or business). Specific documents like National ID and proof of income will be required.
How long does the approval process take? expand_more
Most applications are reviewed within 24 hours. Once approved, funds are typically disbursed to your account on the same day or the next business day.
Are there any hidden fees? expand_more
No. We pride ourselves on transparency. All interest rates, processing fees, and repayment terms are clearly outlined in your loan agreement before you sign.
Can I settle my loan early? expand_more
Yes, you can settle your loan early. We encourage responsible financial management and do not penalize for early repayment on most of our products.
